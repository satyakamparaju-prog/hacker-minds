📧 Phishing

Deceptive messages try to make users click, open content or reveal information. Urgency, unknown senders and suspicious links are warning signs.

🔑 Password Risk

Weak or reused credentials increase account risk. Unique passwords and multi-factor authentication improve protection.

🦠 Malware

Unsafe files or downloads can introduce malicious software. Avoid unexpected content and verify its source.

How We Defend

🛡 Prevent

Unique passwords, MFA, updates, safe browsing and trusted sources.

🔎 Detect

Look for suspicious messages, unusual logins and unexpected files.

🚨 Respond

Stop interacting, report suspicious content and reset compromised credentials when necessary.

♻ Recover

Use backups, restore safely, review the incident and improve controls.
