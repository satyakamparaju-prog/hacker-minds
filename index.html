📧 Phishing

Deceptive messages try to make users click, open content or reveal information. Urgency, unknown senders and suspicious links are warning signs.

🔑 Password Risk

Weak or reused credentials increase account risk. Unique passwords and multi-factor authentication improve protection.

🦠 Malware

Unsafe files or downloads can introduce malicious software. Avoid unexpected content and verify its source.

How We Defend Against Cyber Attacks

Our defense model follows four stages: Prevent → Detect → Respond → Recover. The goal is to reduce the chance of an attack succeeding and limit its impact if something goes wrong.

🛡 Prevent

  • Use strong, unique passwords.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA).
  • Keep OS, apps and browsers updated.
  • Download software only from trusted sources.

🔎 Detect

  • Check sender addresses and suspicious URLs.
  • Watch for unusual login alerts or account activity.
  • Be cautious with unexpected attachments and pop-ups.
  • Use security tools and alerts where available.

🚨 Respond

  • Stop interacting with the suspicious content.
  • Report phishing or suspicious activity.
  • Change affected passwords from a trusted device.
  • Follow your organization's incident-reporting process.

♻ Recover

  • Restore affected systems or data from trusted backups.
  • Review what happened and close the weakness.
  • Monitor accounts for further suspicious activity.
  • Improve security practices after the incident.

Safety Precautions — What Should We Do?

These practical precautions are the core user-safety guidance demonstrated by our prototype.

📧 If it is Phishing

Warning signs: urgency, unknown sender, suspicious link, unexpected request.

Precaution: Do not click. Verify the request using an independent trusted channel and report the message.

🔑 If it is a Password Risk

Warning signs: reused passwords, weak passwords, unexpected login alerts.

Precaution: Use a unique strong password, enable MFA and never share passwords or OTPs.

🦠 If it may be Malware

Warning signs: unexpected attachments, unknown downloads, suspicious pop-ups.

Precaution: Do not open or install it. Verify the source and report it if appropriate.

🌐 Everyday Protection

Remember: update software, use trusted websites and apps, back up important data, lock devices and think before you click.

🚨 If You Think You Were Attacked

Stop → Disconnect/close the suspicious activity → Report → Change affected credentials from a trusted device → Check accounts → Restore safely from trusted backups → Learn and improve.
